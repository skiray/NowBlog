A lot of people do dark mode as `#000` background and `#fff` text, then wonder why their eyes protest.

## Pure black isn't your friend

Pure black with pure white is too high contrast and harsh. I lean on a near-black like `#080a0f` and a soft `#e8ebf2` for text.

## Use hierarchy instead of borders

Heavy borders look dirty on dark. Rather than drawing boxes, float the card up with a subtle brightness difference.

## A touch of blue

Sneak a little blue into the accent and it's easier on the eyes late at night. It's unscientific, but you'll feel it once you try.
