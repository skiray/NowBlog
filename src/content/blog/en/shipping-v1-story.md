If a demo is a love affair, shipping v1.0 is deciding to marry — you're now responsible for how it looks long-term.

## Features I had to cut

Every "do it next version" feature is a rational act of letting go. For v1.0 I kept just three things: write, search, subscribe.

## Naming took longer than coding

The slogan "Ship your product faster" went through eleven revisions. Copy is the other half of a product's face.

## The sigh of relief

Build passes, deploy succeeds, the first real visitor arrives — and in that moment it feels worth it.
