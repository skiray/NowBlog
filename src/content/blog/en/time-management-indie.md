The irony of freelancing: nobody manages you, yet it's easier to waste a whole day.

## Set your own deadlines

No external deadline? Invent one. Even just "ship a post this Friday." A commitment to the outside world is what makes motivation real.

## Save the big blocks for creating

Replies and bug fixes go into one afternoon window. Mornings, when the head is clear, are for the single most important thing.

## Allow idling

Some days the words just won't come — go for a walk. Inspiration often returns the moment you let go.
